# Stakeholder Validation Protocol & Provenance

This document details the stakeholder validation framework, professional archetypes, consent mechanisms, and clinical trial status for SafeDose NearMiss.

---

## 1. Validation Philosophy & Academic Integrity

In compliance with academic evaluation standards:
1. **Zero Fabrication**: No false hospital testimonials, fabricated doctor quotes, or simulated committee stamps are presented as real external endorsements.
2. **Clear Provenance Labels**:
   - Simulated demonstration cards are labeled `SYNTHETIC ARCHETYPE`.
   - Real submissions entered via the evaluation portal are marked `LIVE EVALUATOR` with status `Pending Verification` until confirmed by safety audit leads.
3. **Formal Hospital Trials**: Real-world acute NHS trust trials remain a scheduled milestone subject to clinical governance and ethics review.

---

## 2. Professional Archetypes (Demonstration Profiles)

To model acute hospital user personas during design iterations, the following 4 professional archetypes were synthesized from published ISMP and NHS patient safety literature:

1. **Lead Medication Safety Pharmacist**
   - *Focus*: Standardized taxonomies, category concordance, systemic factor capture.
   - *Key Value*: Structured categorization eliminates free-text ambiguity.
2. **Acute Ward Sister / Band 7 Nurse**
   - *Focus*: Shift velocity, interruption resilience, psychological safety.
   - *Key Value*: 60-second completion time fits into high-pressure ward routines.
3. **Junior Resident Physician**
   - *Focus*: Blame-free near-miss logging, error learning without punitive jeopardy.
   - *Key Value*: Anonymous reporting removes fear of career reprimand.
4. **Director of Clinical Governance & Risk**
   - *Focus*: Trend aggregation, board-level compliance, export durability.
   - *Key Value*: Standardized analytics support immediate root-cause intervention.

---

## 3. Interactive Evaluator Portal

The live prototype at `/validation` incorporates an evaluation form allowing judges, academics, and peer reviewers to:
- Enter genuine evaluator credentials and organizational affiliation.
- Submit quantitative ratings (1–5 Usability, 1–5 Psychological Safety).
- Provide qualitative feedback and observations.
- Explicitly grant consent for evaluation research.
- Review submissions immediately appended to the evaluation dashboard.
