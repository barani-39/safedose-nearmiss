# Genuine External Stakeholder Validation Protocol & Provenance

This document details the stakeholder validation framework, professional archetypes, consent mechanisms, and clinical trial status for SafeDose NearMiss.

---

## 1. Validation Philosophy & Academic Integrity

In compliance with academic evaluation standards:
1. **Zero Fabrication**: No false hospital testimonials, fabricated doctor quotes, or simulated committee stamps are presented as real external endorsements.
2. **Clear Provenance Labels**:
   - Simulated demonstration cards are labeled `Synthetic Archetype (Demo)`.
   - Real submissions entered via the evaluation portal are marked `Live Evaluator` with status `Pending Verification` until confirmed by safety audit leads.
3. **Requirement Clarity**: The current immediate requirement is **GENUINE EXTERNAL STAKEHOLDER VALIDATION** (e.g. feedback from real nurses, pharmacists, physicians, or safety professionals) rather than a formal inpatient clinical trial.
4. **Current Status**:
   ```
   REAL STAKEHOLDER VALIDATION STILL REQUIRED
   ```
   Zero verified external submissions have been validated yet. The interactive portal (`/validation`) and printable paper protocol are fully operational to receive and record live external clinician reviews.

## 2. Professional Archetypes (Demonstration Profiles)

To model acute hospital user personas during design iterations, the following 4 professional archetypes were synthesized from published medication safety literature:

1. **Lead Medication Safety Pharmacist**
   - *Focus*: Standardized taxonomies, category concordance, systemic factor capture.
   - *Key Value*: Structured categorization eliminates free-text ambiguity.
2. **Acute Ward Sister / Band 7 Nurse**
   - *Focus*: Shift velocity, interruption resilience, psychological safety.
   - *Key Value*: 60-second completion time fits into high-pressure ward routines.
3. **Junior Resident Physician**
   - *Focus*: Blame-free near-miss logging, error learning without punitive jeopardy.
   - *Key Value*: Anonymous reporting removes fear of career reprimand.
4. **Director of Clinical Governance & Risk**
   - *Focus*: Trend aggregation, board-level compliance, export durability.
   - *Key Value*: Standardized analytics support immediate root-cause intervention.

## 3. Interactive Evaluator Portal & Paper Protocol

The live prototype incorporates two validation pathways:

1. **Digital Submission Form** (`/validation`):
   - Real clinicians, judges, and safety officers can input name, role, organization, and department.
   - 1–5 Usability rating & 1–5 Psychological safety rating.
   - Qualitative feedback and observations.
   - Mandatory consent acknowledgement.
   - Immediate recording with status `Pending Verification`.

2. **Printable Paper Protocol**:
   - Printable 1-page physical evaluation form for bedside and ward clipboard review.
   - Accessible via the "Print Evaluation Form as PDF" button.
